What's different about implementing an AI marketing platform
Output depends on customer inputsAim and Agents are only as good as the prompts and context the customer provides. Weak inputs mean weak output, and the customer blames the product.
Value is invisible without ITVisibility moves inside AI answers. Without Agent Analytics, the customer can't tie their work to traffic on their own site.
The ground keeps movingAnswer engines change and the prompts that matter drift. Setup is a loop (SAGE), not a one-time task.
AI output needs trustAgents re-reason each run, so output varies. Teams adopt it once review steps fit how they already publish.
Close these three first
1One tracker, one definition of success

Every other fix depends on it. Without the same steps, owners and done criteria for every account, I can't see stalls across dozens of accounts, and there's no baseline to prove ROI against.

2Inputs done by day 14

Prompt set approved, Brand Kit reviewed in Context Manager, Agent Analytics connected. These are the three stalls in the brief, and all three sit on the customer's side. Profound drafts first, the customer approves, and nobody starts from a blank page.

3Plan for the habit cliff

About 40% stall right after first value, when the keys are handed over. So handover becomes a phase (days 57 to 84) with a set weekly routine and fading coaching, not a single moment.

2.2The redesigned process

Live time goes where judgment or buy-in is needed. Everything that's just information goes async.

Live 1:1Only for decisions specific to one customer: goals, owners, their prompts, their results.
Live group (cohorts)Customers starting the same month learn the same skills together. Same quality, a fraction of the hours.
Async and self-serveGuides, templates, short videos and University. Customers move at their own pace, and nothing waits on a meeting.

Two layersEach customer gets a shared tracker with their owners and dates, like Nike's. Every account then rolls up into one internal board in the CS or CRM tool the team already uses, so EMs see the same view. Saved views: overdue, waiting on customer, checkpoints this week. I review red and amber every Monday.

Where AI takes work off my plate
Starter prompt setsDrafted from the customer's site, industry and past deployments. I review instead of writing.
Follow-up sequencesCheckpoint emails and nudges drafted per account from tracker status, so every customer gets the same follow-ups.
Call notes to trackerRecaps and next actions drafted from call notes, then the tracker updated.
Daily health digestA scheduled AI job reads usage and tracker data and flags accounts that hit a trigger, so I start each day with a short list.
The 48 hours after an account stalls
0hTrigger fires

For example: website not connected by day 10, prompts not approved by day 10, Brand Kit not reviewed by day 14, no logins for 7 days, a missed working session, or the champion leaving.

24hDiagnose and make it easy

Find which step stalled and whose it is. Message the champion with one small, specific ask, not "just checking in." Offer to take work off them: "I'll join a 15-minute call with IT" or "I've drafted the prompts, can you approve by Friday?"

48hEscalate or reset

No reply: go to the second contact, and bring in the EM or budget owner. If the cause is structural, like a new champion, reset owners and dates in the tracker so the plan stays real.
