1.3AEO: making your content agent-ready

Key Idea

The agent can only use what it actually accesses and understands. So you're not marketing to a human, but to the browsing layer that assembles the answer. This is AEO: Answer Engine Optimization.

Search engine · SEO

Rank in a list of links

What the person sees
Ten blue links
Who reads your page first
People
What winning means
Ranking high. Ranking fifth still gets you clicks.
Where the decision happens
On your site, after the click
What to measure
Rankings and clicks
vs
Answer engine · AEO

Be part of one answer

What the person sees
One written answer, with a few sources
Who reads your page first
Agents, then people
What winning means
One answer. You're either in it, or you're invisible.
Where the decision happens
Inside the chat, before any click
What to measure
Visibility, citations, sentiment, AI referral traffic

The three gates every Nike page has to pass

So what does the agent actually need from your page?

1Fetchable
Gate 1Can the answer engine even reach the page and ingest it?
→
2Chosen
Gate 2Does the cover, meaning the URL, title and snippet, look like the answer?
→
3Extractable
Gate 3When it opens the page, can it lift a clean chunk?

Miss any one gate and Nike isn't in the answer. Your editorial, product copy and blog are exactly the pages going through them.

So the question becomes: which questions matter, which gates are you failing, and how do you fix them? That's exactly what Profound does.
